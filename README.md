# BhashaTutor
BhashaTutor is an AI-powered English learning platform designed to help users improve their English speaking, pronunciation, grammar, and communication skills. It allows users to record or upload their voice, convert speech into text, receive AI-powered grammar corrections, understand mistakes, and practise English through interactive AI conversations.

With support for English, Hindi, and Marathi, BhashaTutor makes English learning more accessible and practical for learners.

**Think in your language. Speak confidently in English.**
